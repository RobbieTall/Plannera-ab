Byron Shire Development Control Plan 2014

Chapter B4 Traffic, Parking and Access

This chapter sets out the transport, access, parking and circulation controls that apply to development in Byron Shire. It should be read with Australian Standards AS 2890 and the relevant road authority guidelines.

Controls respond to development intensity, ensure safe movement for all users and protect streetscape amenity through integrated design of driveways, landscaping and parking areas.

B4.1 Application and objectives

Applies to all development requiring on-site parking, loading or access works on public or private land.

Objectives include providing clear and legible access, minimising conflict between vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists, and ensuring parking supply matches demand for the land use.

B4.1.1 Performance approach

Alternative solutions must demonstrate equal or better safety and efficiency outcomes compared to the Acceptable Solutions in this chapter.

B4.1.2 Design references

Refer to AS 2890.1 for car parking design, AS 2890.2 for commercial vehicle access and the Austroads Guide to Road Design for sight distance requirements.

B4.1.3 Accessibility

All car parks must include accessible spaces designed to AS 2890.6 and connected by an accessible path to building entries.

B4.1.4 Transport hierarchy

Site planning should prioritise walking and cycling before private vehicles, with bicycle parking provided near entrances and street frontage.

B4.2 Parking rates

Parking rates are calculated per dwelling or per 100 m2 GFA as nominated. Where a land use is not listed, prepare a traffic and parking study using RMS Guide to Traffic Generating Developments.

Visitor parking must be accessible without passing through locked gates. Tandem parking is only permitted for single dwellings and secondary dwellings sharing the same driveway.

B4.2.1 Residential parking table

Apply the residential rates in Table B4.1 for dwelling houses, dual occupancies, multi dwelling housing and secondary dwellings.

B4.2.2 Mixed use

Where dwellings are above ground floor commercial uses, apply the higher of residential or commercial rates to avoid under-provision.

  • Short-stay accommodation within residential buildings must provide at least one space per bedroom.
  • Management plans must address visitor parking arrangements during peak tourist periods.

B4.2.3 Motorcycle and bicycle parking

Provide one motorcycle space per 20 car spaces and bicycle parking at a rate of one space per dwelling plus visitor rails near the main entrance.

B4.2.4 Car share provision

For developments with more than 40 dwellings, convert one visitor space to a dedicated car share bay for every 25 dwellings above the threshold.

B4.3 Access design

Design access to minimise conflicts and protect existing street trees. Where two driveways are proposed they must be separated by at least 1 m of landscaped verge.

B4.3.1 Driveway width

Residential driveways are between 3.0 m and 5.5 m wide at the property boundary. Wider crossings require demonstration of vehicle swept paths.

B4.3.2 Gradient and transitions

Maximum driveway grade is 20% with 2 m transitions at the property boundary and basement entries to protect vehicle undercarriages.

B4.3.3 Sight lines

Provide sight splays measuring 2.5 m by 2.5 m at residential driveways clear of obstructions above 600 mm height.

B4.3.4 Service vehicle access

Waste collection vehicles must enter and leave in a forward direction for developments of 10 or more dwellings, unless Council approves on-street collection.

B4.3.5 Rural lots

On rural road frontages, provide a minimum 6 m throat depth with all weather surface to prevent tracking of gravel onto the carriageway.

B4.4 Parking layout and design

Parking areas must be efficient, safe and well lit. Basement car parks should incorporate openings for natural ventilation and avoid blank walls at street level.

B4.4.1 Aisle widths

Provide a minimum aisle width of 6.0 m for 90° parking, 5.4 m for 75° and 4.2 m for 60° layouts. Wider aisles are required where columns reduce clear width.

B4.4.2 Headroom

Minimum clear headroom is 2.2 m for cars, 2.4 m for accessible spaces and 3.5 m where small rigid vehicles are permitted.

B4.4.3 Basement security

Install security grilles to separate resident and visitor parking in basements, with intercom access for visitors and delivery drivers.

B4.4.4 Landscaping in parking courts

Provide one medium canopy tree for every six at-grade spaces and landscape islands at row ends to manage stormwater.

B4.4.5 Electric vehicle readiness

New multi-dwelling developments must provide spare capacity in the main switchboard to enable future EV charging of at least 50% of resident spaces.

B4.4.6 Tandem spaces

Tandem parking is permitted for the same dwelling only and must be at least 11 m long with independent access to visitor spaces.

B4.5 Loading and servicing

Provide loading and delivery areas clear of public footpaths. Designs must show vehicle swept paths for the largest vehicle expected to serve the site.

B4.5.1 Waste collection

Where on-site collection is provided, the waste vehicle standing area must be level (max 1% grade) and located to minimise reversing movements.

B4.5.2 Deliveries in mixed use centres

Provide a dedicated loading bay for developments with more than 500 m2 of ground floor retail, located to avoid conflicts with pedestrian desire lines.

B4.5.3 Rural tourism

Short-term accommodation in rural zones must provide an all-weather service bay suitable for a small rigid vehicle near reception areas.

B4.6 Travel demand management

Large developments must encourage mode shift away from private vehicles by incorporating end-of-trip facilities, travel information and management plans.

B4.6.1 Green travel plans

Developments with more than 20 dwellings or 1,000 m2 GFA must submit a green travel plan outlining measures to reduce single-occupancy car trips by at least 10% over five years.

B4.6.2 End-of-trip facilities

Provide secure bicycle storage, lockers and showers for staff in non-residential developments exceeding 1,000 m2 GFA.

B4.6.3 Wayfinding

Install signage showing pedestrian and cycling routes to nearby centres and public transport stops.
