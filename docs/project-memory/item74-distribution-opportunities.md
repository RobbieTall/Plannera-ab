## Purpose

Record promising distribution ideas without diverting delivery from the evidence-confirmed Byron Pathway Check.

The product foundation remains one shared, versioned evidence graph rendered as:

- a free Pathway Check;
- an exact-scope A$49 Planning Controls Pack; and
- an A$749 submission SEE candidate with operator controls.

Distribution experiments must not precede trustworthy site-specific outputs.

## Real-estate listing QR concept

A property-specific, co-branded QR code could open a mobile Pathway Check with the listing address already selected.

Recommended customer promise:

> Explore preliminary planning pathways for this property.

Do not promise that a buyer can build a particular proposal. The output must use deterministic `STOP`, `PROCEED`, `MERIT_ASSESSMENT` and `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` decisions backed by current evidence.

### Agent value

- generate more buyer engagement from signboards, brochures and open homes;
- capture the intended property use behind an enquiry;
- differentiate a listing and vendor presentation;
- re-engage buyers who are not ready to call;
- reduce repetitive preliminary planning questions; and
- report measurable scans, completed checks and consented leads.

### Proposed user flow

1. Agent creates a listing-specific Plannera QR code.
2. Buyer opens a co-branded mobile page.
3. The public property is preselected.
4. Buyer chooses an intended project such as a shed, extension, secondary dwelling or pool.
5. Plannera returns free preliminary pathway guidance.
6. Buyer may purchase the A$49 controls pack or progress toward the A$749 SEE.
7. Buyer separately chooses whether to share their contact details and intended use with the agent.

Consent to agent contact must be separate from obtaining the planning result.

### Initial commercial model

Start without referral payments.

- The agent receives engagement and consented leads.
- The vendor receives a differentiated campaign.
- The buyer receives useful preliminary guidance.
- Plannera retains revenue from its own paid products.

Avoiding referral commissions initially reduces conflicts, disclosure complexity and partner resistance.

### Advertising and disclosure guardrails

Real-estate advertising must not misrepresent the characteristics of land or the use to which it may lawfully be put.

Relevant references:

- NSW Fair Trading real-estate advertising guidance: https://www.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au/trades-and-businesses/business-essentials/advertising-and-marketing-your-business/real-estate-agent-advertisements
- NSW Fair Trading property advertising guidelines: https://www.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-property/property-professionals/working-as-a-property-agent/advertising-guidelines
- NSW Property and Stock Agents Regulation 2022: https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/sl-2022-0501
- ACCC real-estate guidance: https://www.accc.gov.au/business/specific-products-and-activities/real-estate

Plannera must own the result wording, evidence date, limitations and escalation path. A disclaimer alone is insufficient.

## Higher-intent partner channel

Shed suppliers, secondary-dwelling suppliers and building designers may be an even stronger early partner channel.

Their customer already has a proposed development and planning uncertainty directly blocks a sale. An embedded Pathway Check could:

- qualify the site before detailed quoting;
- identify missing evidence early;
- reduce unsuitable enquiries;
- convert viable enquiries into design or supply work; and
- feed the same evidence into Plannera's paid outputs.

## Recommended channel order

1. Building designers, shed suppliers and secondary-dwelling suppliers for high-intent conversion.
2. Real-estate QR partnerships for reach and address-specific lead capture.
3. Conveyancers and buyer advocates for trusted pre-purchase due diligence.
