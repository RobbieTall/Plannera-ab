# Practitioner Workflow Intelligence and Source Governance

Status: Product-governance standard  
Last updated: September 2026

## Purpose

Plannera may learn from real practitioner workflows, checklists, assessment sequences and operational methods, but must not mistake them for statutory authority or a universal professional methodology.

The initial Byron practitioner material in Drive was developed from practical assessment experience with the kinds of applications commonly handled by an entry-level/local-government planner. It is useful operational evidence. It is not asserted to represent the complete practice of experienced planners working on larger, higher-value, regionally significant, integrated, designated or otherwise complex development.

## Source hierarchy

### 1. Authoritative planning sources

Current legislation, SEPPs, LEPs, DCPs, regulations, adopted policies, official planning maps, responsible-authority datasets, current council forms and other legally or administratively authoritative material.

These establish what applies.

### 2. Professional assessment frameworks

Broader planning methodology derived from legislation, established professional practice, high-quality assessment reports, guidance and validated input from planners and specialists with relevant experience.

These inform how issues should be analysed and documented.

### 3. Practitioner workflow intelligence

Practical workflows, checklists, assessment sequences, recurring issue lists and administrative methods developed through real practice.

These are heuristic inputs that can help Plannera decide what to investigate, what may commonly be missed and how to organise an assessment. They do not independently establish that a control, constraint, approval pathway or referral requirement applies.

## Core rule

**Practitioner workflow intelligence may inform what Plannera checks; authoritative sources determine what Plannera says.**

A practitioner item must not be promoted into a statutory conclusion unless independently supported by a current authoritative source or clearly labelled professional judgement.

## Initial Byron practitioner reference pack

The Drive folder Planning Docs / Byron Info Docs is an early practitioner reference pack, not a canonical statutory dataset.

It contains practical assessment matrices, DA assessment/admin workflow notes, exempt/complying-development checklists and guides, council determination/report procedures and owner-consent material.

Useful product signals include:

- recurring assessment sequencing;
- practical map/layer coverage used during assessment;
- evidence and document inputs commonly needed before confident assessment;
- common RFI, report, delegate and determination workflow stages;
- lodgement/readiness requirements such as owner consent;
- user-facing workflow patterns for exempt and complying development.

Do not describe the pack as “the planner's perspective”, “Council's complete assessment method” or a universal checklist.

## Complexity-aware assessment

Plannera should maintain a common assessment core and expand it according to proposal scale, statutory pathway, environmental constraints, specialist triggers and decision-making complexity.

Indicative internal orchestration tiers:

1. **Routine / small development** — targeted statutory/DCP controls, common mapped constraints, concise impact assessment and simple readiness checks.
2. **Moderate complexity** — broader site/locality analysis, additional mapped constraints, specialist triggers, referrals, variations and more developed effects analysis.
3. **Complex / specialist** — multidisciplinary evidence, significant constraints, detailed merit reasoning, multiple approvals/referrals, specialist-report reconciliation and explicit uncertainty management.
4. **Major / regional / integrated / designated or equivalent** — bespoke statutory/procedural pathway, higher-order specialist and agency involvement, panel/authority considerations and stronger human professional review.

These tiers are product orchestration concepts, not legal classifications.

## Product application

### Quick Site Check and Planning Controls Pack
Use practitioner intelligence to improve issue-spotting and completeness checks. Never convert a practitioner checklist itself into a legal rule set.

### Research Viewer
Use practitioner material as one input to coverage testing for layers a working planner may consult. Each layer relied on must still be traced to its responsible authority and checked for publisher, currency, coverage, scale and legal relevance.

### SEE Builder
Use practitioner intelligence to anticipate assessment issues and improve structure, while the SEE remains grounded in the canonical SEE Builder standard, current statutory sources, project evidence and specialist reports. The goal is to make assessment easier without assuming every planner assesses every proposal identically.

### Council Edition
Use operational material such as RFI, assessment, delegate and determination workflows as workflow research only. Validate across councils, senior planners and more complex application types before generalising.

### Exempt / CDC pathway
Use checklists and explanatory guides as workflow/UX references. Eligibility must always be resolved from the current Codes SEPP and other authoritative instruments.

## Validation and learning model

Progressively strengthen this layer by:

- comparing workflows across councils and development types;
- reviewing assessment reports and determination patterns;
- obtaining input from experienced planners and relevant specialists;
- recording where workflows differ by scale, tenure, pathway, geography and consent authority;
- testing whether additional checks improve assessment quality rather than adding boilerplate;
- retaining provenance that distinguishes statutory fact, professional methodology and practitioner heuristic.

## Design principle

The goal is not to automate one planner's habits. The goal is to learn from real planning practice while building a broader, evidence-led and complexity-aware assessment system that remains accurate, adaptable and professionally defensible.
