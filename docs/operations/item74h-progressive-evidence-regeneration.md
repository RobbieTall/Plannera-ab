## Authority and currency

- `AUTHORITATIVE_CONTROL`: current legislation, official mapping or current Council control material.
- `OFFICIAL_CASE_RECORD`: a determination or stamped plan proving what happened in that case.
- `PROFESSIONAL_REPORT`: survey or specialist evidence that must be checked for scope, date, authorship and limitations.
- `SECONDARY_PUBLIC_RECORD`: submitted SEE, plan or report whose claims require current authoritative replay.

Case history never becomes current law merely because Council published it. A submitted SEE cannot override a current LEP, DCP or authoritative map.

## Public DA History Assist boundary

The Byron adapter accepts a matching HTTPS tracker URL and application number, lists allow-listed document metadata, classifies likely evidentiary value, and requires explicit customer selection.

It does not:

- crawl unrelated properties;
- claim the tracker is a complete property history;
- copy Council documents automatically;
- return private object URLs;
- treat a previous approval as a promise for the current proposal;
- use historical controls without current replay.

Until written permission or a permitted integration exists, import is link-only or customer-supplied upload into private project storage. Discovery is cheap; OCR and extraction occur only after selection and within paid or explicitly approved processing.

## Gate resolution

A legal boundary/set-out gap can be resolved only by accepted, current professional survey evidence that is privately stored, cleanly scanned and operator reviewed.

A final control-currency gap can be resolved only by an accepted, current authoritative control replay.

Pending, rejected, expired, conflicting or secondary public evidence preserves the gap. Even after those two gates resolve, this proof regenerates working products only and never asserts submission readiness.
