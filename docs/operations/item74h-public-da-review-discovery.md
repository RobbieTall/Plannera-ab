After the public DA quarantine gate passes, the temporary review-discovery gate
refetches the exact allowlisted Byron records, repeats private Blob quarantine
and ClamAV scanning, and extracts only bounded page-labelled lines matching
role-specific planning terms.

The discovery output is evidence for an operator to inspect. It is not itself an
operator decision. A candidate line cannot establish authority, a numeric
measurement, a road classification or a survey-to-layout relationship without
the surrounding page being assessed under the existing review contract.

The gate never retains a PDF or extracted text, never logs council download
capabilities or content hashes, never creates a paid artefact binding, and must
delete every private Blob object, sandbox and snapshot before passing.
Production checkout remains disabled.
